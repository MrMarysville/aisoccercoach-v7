# Independent calibration audit — 2026-09-10

A fresh agent audited an isolated checkout and the frozen private evidence.
The findings below distinguish code defects, measured limitations and proposed
experiments. No reserved footage was opened and no acceptance gate was weakened.
The complete report, reproducible checks and numerical receipts are private under
`out/fresh-calibration-audit-20260910/`.

## Findings

1. **Assembly can rebind reviewed paint to a different exact frame.** The
   [assembler](tools/assemble_field_recovery.py) checked source-video/review hashes
   but looked up only the frame index before copying PTS and image identity from
   the supplied manifest. A synthetic reproducer successfully attached paint
   from PTS 9000/image A to PTS 18000/image B, despite a stale split-manifest hash.
   The sampler also omitted exact identities from its reference records. The
   shared input boundary needs exact-frame and split-manifest validation while
   retaining the intended annotation-subset workflow. The audit separately
   verified all current Granite references/boundaries and all three Butte remaps;
   **none of those current inputs was corrupted**.
   **Post-audit repair:** exact identities and split hashes are now validated,
   legacy annotation subsets remain supported through their verified manifests,
   and remaps preserve fitting roles. The full 207-test Python suite passes.

2. **Opposite projective branches dominate Granite's geometry failures.** Across
   all 1,611 frames, the audit reproduced 301,990 folded grid samples; 301,947
   involve an active chart's opposite orientation branch. There are still **43
   folds on positive branches across 19 frames**. Also, 191,724 folded samples
   lie inside the declared support hulls, so excluding unsupported points alone
   would not solve the problem. On ten probed frames, removing temporal/spatial
   boundary corrections or choosing a linear boundary taper did not change the
   fold counts. A source-established physical branch domain is a proposed
   experiment, not a validated fix or permission to suppress warnings.

3. **Reference qualification should precede long propagation.** Granite's
   midfield reference already has a halfway training p95 of 7.837 px, before the
   488.221-second motion/fit run and 136.204-second boundary refinement. Butte's
   first fresh midfield fit correctly rejects rank below eight: circle and
   halfway evidence alone do not determine its homography. Acquire another
   independently identified marking or a better complementary source view;
   retain the rank and error thresholds.
   **Post-audit source work:** eight reviewed far-line points resolve Butte's
   missing midfield constraint. A 15.944-second preflight now passes every local
   reference's training target, with maximum p95 2.584 px. Temporal boundary
   evidence is still insufficient; this does not qualify either minute.

4. **Fragmentation changes fitting weights.** The [residual objective](field_recovery.py)
   normalizes each annotation fragment separately. Splitting identical paint
   points into two fragments doubles their total squared weight. A bounded
   diagnostic refit grouping unchanged midfield points by physical marking took
   5.632 seconds and reduced the long-halfway p95 to 6.458 px, while the shorter
   portion worsened to 6.069 px. It still fails. Any correction should preserve
   fragment provenance and prove partition invariance before refitting candidates.

5. **Support and evidence completeness limit qualification.** Granite's midfield
   support hull reaches only x=4.549 m, while its fixed blend contributes through
   x<38 m for y<30 m. Unobserved support cannot be enlarged to fill that gap.
   The frozen forward/backward checks retain 154/137 incomplete groups, so the
   maximum complete-evidence counts are currently 140/294 and 133/270. Fresh v7
   passes 119/294 and 81/270, versus polynomial 112/294 and 53/270, but near-line
   checks regress to 0/28 and 0/27. Preserve those per-marking failures alongside
   aggregate gains.

6. **Upload-triggered Codex processing remains unbuilt.** The app opens prepared
   clips and supports review/save/lookup. Agent-performed browser markup does
   not demonstrate upload → actual Codex invocation → calibration processing.
   A future local job can reuse the current preparation, annotation, measurement,
   atlas and scoring contracts without requiring a new hosted architecture.

## Method scope and next steps

The saved historical v7 evaluator was reproduced exactly, and the new control
uses its three-chart/motion/boundary architecture. Its fitting objective is not
an exact replay of the historical recipe: normalization, finite extents,
fragment weighting and selected near-side evidence differ. The useful historical
preview did not certify every local marking at the newer 3/6 px targets.

First repair the assembly identity boundary, with regression checks for changed
PTS/images, stale splits, legitimate subsets and explicit exact-source remaps.
Then run short reference/coverage checks and a separately declared branch-domain
experiment, keeping all 43 positive-branch folds and every lost region explicit.
The upload/Codex job remains a later integration task; it must preserve immutable
artifacts, model metadata, resume behavior, stale-write rejection and the same
calibration gates.

This audit did not change code, maps, owner revisions or source roles. Physical
metric certification and full-game acceptance remain false. Subsequent fixes
and measured results are recorded in [CURRENT-WORK.md](../CURRENT-WORK.md).
